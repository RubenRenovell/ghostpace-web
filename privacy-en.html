1. Who we are

GhostPace (“we”, “the App”) is a sports analysis and 3D replay product operated from Spain and subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: ghostpace.app@gmail.com.

Your training data is yours. We do not sell, rent, or hand your health, GPS, or performance information to advertisers.

2. Data we receive from Strava

GhostPace connects to your Strava account with read-only OAuth scopes (read, activity:read_all). We collect:

  • Basic profile: Strava athlete ID and public name, to create your GhostPace account.
  • Activity and telemetry: route files (.FIT or equivalent) including GPS, heart rate, cadence, power, pace, and elevation, only for activities Strava allows us to read under your Strava privacy settings.

GhostPace does not post, edit, or delete activities on Strava on your behalf. We do not write to your Strava feed.

3. How we use that data

Strava data is used only inside GhostPace, for your own activities:

  • Replay and 3D visualisation of sessions you imported.
  • Compare efforts you choose — typically other laps you own on the same circuit — not a public race or third-party league.
  • Group your routes into habitual “Circuits” and show effort metrics (pace, elevation, time) on those sessions.

We do not use this data for targeted ads, commercial profiling, or ad networks.

4. Storage and processors

Processors:

  • Supabase: auth and light metadata, with row-level security.
  • Cloudflare R2: private telemetry objects (JSON / FIT), not a public index.
  • Render: the app API (sync and session delivery).

5. Beta support access

During closed beta, engineering may use read-only access to processed data to fix a critical failure or when you ask for help. We do not copy your routes out of that debug context.

6. Disconnect and deletion

Revoke access

Disconnect anytime in the GhostPace profile (“Disconnect Strava”) or in Strava on the web: Settings > My Apps, then “Revoke Access” next to GhostPace. If you revoke access or delete an activity on Strava, we stop syncing and honour the webhook to purge the associated copy.

Full deletion

Email ghostpace.app@gmail.com with subject “Deletion request”. Within 30 days we permanently delete your user and associated files (.FIT / .JSON). This cannot be undone.
