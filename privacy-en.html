1. Data we receive from Strava

After you tap Connect with Strava, we use read-only OAuth scopes read and activity:read_all:

  • Basic profile needed to bind your GhostPace account (Strava athlete ID, public name).
  • Activity telemetry Strava allows us to read under your Strava privacy settings: GPS, time, elevation, heart rate, cadence, power when present.

We do not post, edit, or delete activities on Strava. We do not request email or write scopes.

2. How we use it

Only inside GhostPace, for you:

  • 3D replay of sessions you imported.
  • Compare a session with another lap you own on the same circuit (Ghost).
  • Pace: a derived, same-effort clock of this session. Not another athlete.

We do not use Strava data for ads, commercial profiling, or ad networks. We do not train AI models on your Strava data.

Strava may collect usage data about our use of the API, as described in the Strava API Agreement.

3. Storage

  • Supabase: account metadata and light catalog fields (row-level security). Not full GPS JSON in the feed.
  • Cloudflare R2: private derived session objects (JSON). Raw pending streams expire in 7 days.
  • Render: API (sync and session URLs).
  • Mapbox: map tiles on your device.

The replay file is a downsampled, relative-time derived work, not a 1:1 copy of Strava streams. Strava activity IDs are not shown in the app; “View on Strava” uses a public URL when you choose it.

4. Who sees your data

Only you, on your signed-in GhostPace session. We do not show your Strava data to other athletes.

5. Withdrawal and deletion

Disconnect Strava

In Profile, or Strava → Settings → My Apps → Revoke GhostPace: we delete our copy of your Strava activities immediately and show a confirmation ID. Email that ID to ghostpace.app@gmail.com for a written copy.

Delete an activity on Strava

We purge our copy (webhook; within 48 hours, usually seconds).

Delete account

In Profile: we delete your GhostPace user and remaining files. Nothing is deleted on Strava.com. You also get a confirmation ID.

Legal maximum for completing erasure is 30 days where a residual processor delay exists; the in-app action is immediate.

6. Access to your GhostPace copy

Open the app while signed in. For a file export of our derived copy, email ghostpace.app@gmail.com. Strava’s own bulk export remains on Strava.com.

7. Children

GhostPace is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If the types of Strava data we collect change, we will ask you to authorize again.
